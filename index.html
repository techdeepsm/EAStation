Frequently Asked Questions

Do these algorithms work with Prop Firms?

Yes. Every EA is designed with stringent risk parameters, hard stop losses, and dynamic trailing that aligns with the rules of major proprietary trading firms (FTMO, FundedNext, etc.).

Do I need a VPS?

Yes, to run these algorithms properly, you will need a Virtual Private Server (VPS) to keep MetaTrader 5 running 24/5 (or 24/7 for BTCSniperV2) without relying on your home PC.

How do I purchase a license?

Click the purchase button on any EA's specific page. It will open an email directly to me (deepsmpatra@gmail.com) where we will securely process your MT5 account number locking and license delivery.

Which broker do you recommend?

While these algorithms work on any MT5 broker with low spreads, we highly recommend Vantage Markets, IC Markets, or any true raw spread ECN broker to minimize slippage on high-frequency scalp entries.

What is the maximum drawdown?

Each system is hard-coded with a max daily drawdown limit (usually 2-3%). If this limit is breached, the EA automatically halts trading for the day to protect your capital and ensure you easily maintain your funded prop firm accounts.
